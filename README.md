## About Me

Hi, I'm **Pratibha Patel**, a Data Science graduate who enjoys exploring data, discovering patterns, and turning complex information into insights that are easier to understand and act on.

My interest lies at the intersection of data analysis and business decision-making. I use SQL to investigate datasets, Python to clean and process information, Excel to organize and analyze data, and Power BI to build interactive dashboards that communicate performance clearly.

Through hands-on projects, I've explored retail sales and customer behavior, IPL performance across seasons, YouTube content engagement, and hospital operational reporting. These projects have helped me practice the complete analytical workflow—from understanding the problem and validating data to writing queries, building dashboards, and communicating findings.

### 01. YouTube Content Performance Analytics

<div align="center">

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3776AB?style=flat-square&logo=python&logoColor=white" />
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Pandas-150458?style=flat-square&logo=pandas&logoColor=white" />

**Objective:** Investigate which content characteristics are associated with stronger video reach and audience engagement.

- **Data acquisition and quality:** Retrieved approximately 2,400 raw video records through the YouTube Data API v3, then cleaned and deduplicated the dataset to retain **2,092 unique videos**. Used Pandas and validation rules to prepare the data for analysis.
- **Comparative SQL analysis:** Compared short-form and long-form content using SQL queries to investigate relationships between video duration, views, and engagement.
- **Performance exploration:** Examined top-performing videos and duration-versus-views trends to understand how content characteristics relate to performance.
- **Dashboard development:** Built an interactive Power BI dashboard to make video-level comparisons and performance patterns easier to explore.
- **Analytical application:** Designed the reporting workflow to help content creators and marketers investigate performance and make more informed content decisions.

**Objective:** Analyze ball-by-ball IPL data to compare team performance, batting contributions, and bowling trends across seasons.

- **Large-scale data processing:** Processed **280,000+ ball-by-ball records** using Python, Pandas, and SQLAlchemy to prepare match-level data for analysis.

- **Performance investigation:** Analyzed team, batting, and bowling trends to explore player contributions and differences in performance across seasons.
- **Dashboard design:** Developed a KPI-driven Power BI dashboard that brings key performance indicators and player/team comparisons into an interactive reporting experience.
- **Analytical value:** Made it easier to explore season-wise trends and compare performance without manually reviewing individual delivery records.
